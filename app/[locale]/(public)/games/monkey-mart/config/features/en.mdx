## Unlocking the Monkey Mart Master Within

*The game throws curve balls! Ever had a customer angrily point at the empty banana shelf? I panicked! After maxing storage and hiring two baboons, my rating swiftly recovered.*

The journey to Monkey Mart tycoon-hood is paved with smart choices. First, automate! Second, maximize the "Prestige" system (a mechanic for resetting and growing further). And if that isn't enough, optimize to the "Merge Mechanics" (combining items to scale your system). What awaits you at the top? A booming business and a legacy of bananas💰.
