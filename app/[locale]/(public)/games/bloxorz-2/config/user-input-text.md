𝐁𝐥𝐨𝐱𝐨𝐫𝐳 𝟐 is the totally unexpected sequel to the 𝐁𝐥𝐨𝐱𝐨𝐫𝐳 series, in which you find your little rectangular block of stone that you will have to take everywhere with you. To unlock the next level, you will have to pass your stone through the hole provided for this purpose. To do this, you will move this cumbersome and impractical object by rotating and falling it. Collect as many stars as possible, make new passages appear in the game and go to the end of this crazy adventure. Good luck to everyone !
