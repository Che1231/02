Mahjong, also known as Mahjongg, is a traditional Chinese tile - based game that has gained popularity worldwide. Here is a detailed introduction:

### Game Components
- **Tiles**: A standard mahjong set consists of 144 tiles, including three suits of numbered tiles (dots, bamboo, and characters), honor tiles (winds and dragons), and sometimes additional special tiles.
- **Dice**: Usually two dice are used to determine the starting position and various game actions.

### Gameplay
- **Initial Setup**: The tiles are shuffled and arranged in a square formation. Each player draws a certain number of tiles to start the game.
- **Objective**: The goal is to form specific combinations of tiles, usually a set of four melds (groups of three or four tiles) and a pair. Different regions and rule sets may have specific winning combinations.
- **Drawing and Discarding**: Players take turns drawing tiles from the wall and discarding unwanted tiles. They can also form melds by taking tiles from the discard pile or from other players' hands under certain conditions.
- **Winning**: When a player successfully forms the required combination, they declare "mahjong" and win the game. The score is calculated based on the difficulty and value of the winning hand.

### Variations
- **Regional Differences**: There are many different regional variations of mahjong in China, such as Shanghai mahjong, Cantonese mahjong, and Sichuan mahjong, each with its own unique rules and characteristics.
- **International Versions**: Outside of China, mahjong has also been adapted and modified in different countries. For example, Japanese mahjong has its own set of rules and scoring systems.

### Cultural Significance
- **Social Activity**: Mahjong is not only a game but also a popular social activity. It is often played among family members, friends, or in social clubs, providing an opportunity for people to interact and communicate.
- **Reflection of Culture**: It reflects many aspects of Chinese culture, including traditional values, number and color symbolism, and strategic thinking. The game requires players to have skills in observation, calculation, and decision - making.
