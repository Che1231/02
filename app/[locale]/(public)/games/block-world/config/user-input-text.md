About: Block World
Play Block World game now!

Block World is a construction game similar to Minecraft style. This is a free game, you are completely free to imagine without any constraints.

Extremely easy construction and control mechanism.

You can choose from 2 game modes: life or freedom.

It plays out like real life, day and night. Very authentic!

The night will have a flashlight to assist you.


 
Instructions
WASD or arrow keys to move
Left-click to remove a block
Right-click to place a block
Space bar to jump
1 to 9 numbers to choose a block
T uses the torch
F to use a flashlight
