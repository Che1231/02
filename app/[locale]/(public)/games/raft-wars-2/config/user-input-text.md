Simon found valuable treasures while playing on the beach. Some bad guys attempted to steal the treasure, but Simon and his brother joined forces to defend it. In Raft Wars 2, Simon and his brother came back from vacation only to discover a water park had been constructed on top of their hidden treasure! Now, they must take action to shut down the water park. Fight against the enemies using a tennis ball gun. Each time you knock down an enemy, you earn points. Enhance your weapons and raft to have a greater effect in the battle.How to PlayIn this game, you'll have to solve a series of physics-based challenges.
