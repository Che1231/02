The third platform game in the Vex series is called Vex 3. The game has many unexpected turns which give players the most exciting gaming experience. Each level of Vex 3 requires you to navigate a maze of hazardous obstacles and traps. Avoiding the spinning blades, spikes, and traps in order to get to the next act.Controls GuideYou must complete each act of Vex 3 if you want to pass through it. You must dodge the lethal traps and equipment placed throughout each level. To move, press [W], [A], [S], [D], or the arrow keys. To climb up walls, all you need to do is jump from side to side. By pressing the down arrow key while standing on a new act's act block, you can play the new one. Since purple blocks are very weak, stepping on them will cause them to fall. Orange blocks are incredibly bouncy and are excellent for high-flying jumps!More Stickman GamesAlmighty stickmen can accompany players in many different genres.
