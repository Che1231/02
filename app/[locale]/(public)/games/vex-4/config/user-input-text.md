Vex 4 is the fourth exciting installment of the Vex game series, offering an even greater challenge. Your goal in Vex 4 is to escape each level by navigating through obstacles with running, jumping, sliding, and even swimming. The gameplay remains familiar, allowing you to utilize various moves like wall climbing. A new addition is the ghost recorder, which lets you record your movements in the levels. It's an optional feature, but it's pretty cool to try out. The game looks and plays pretty much like before, with controls using either WASD or arrow keys based on your preference. Get ready for an exhilarating adventure in Vex 4!
