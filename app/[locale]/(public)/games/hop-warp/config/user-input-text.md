Jump, warp, and conquer in Hop Warp! Navigate treacherous levels, master the art of warping, and become the ultimate interdimensional explorer.Hop Warp is the adventure of a small character in closed mazes. Experience a mind-bending platformer where dimensions collide. You will transform into this small creature to participate in the game. Your mission is to collect all the stars in the round to complete it.Explore Unique GameplayThe core gameplay of Hop Warp is to collect all the stars in the level. Because your character can only jump to a certain height, the new challenge officially begins.In each level, you will have the right to change the character's shape into a sparkling star. In this state, the small creature can move anywhere, including through walls. This is both an opportunity and a challenge for your smart tactical ability. Do not worry! Surely the number of transitions will be just enough for you to complete that level. That also means that you have only one way to win a round.How to ControlMove: the arrow or WASD keys.Change: the X key or the spacebar.Note that red walls and areas do not allow the character to transform.A Special Tip for YouYou should track the number of shape conversions first.
