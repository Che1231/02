Betrayal.io is an engaging multiplayer social deduction game reminiscent of Among Us. In this game, players assume the roles of either crewmates or secret saboteurs known as 'betrayers'. The betrayer's objective is to disrupt operations and create chaos, while the crewmates must diligently fulfill their duties while avoiding elimination. Work together, stay vigilant, and uncover the betrayer's true identity to ensure the success of your crew!
