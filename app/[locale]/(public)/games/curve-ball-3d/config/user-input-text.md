Have you ever tried your hand at a first-person tennis match in a virtual world? This game has just such exciting rounds! Get ready for experiences you can't find anywhere else and practice your ability to concentrate.Gameplay OverviewYou will use a virtual shield, like a racket, to block your opponent's ball and launch a counterattack. To score points, you need to hit the ball in places where the opponent cannot block it. Curve Ball 3D's scoring mechanism is extremely unique. The fixed score for each round is 3,000 and will decrease over time. That means the faster you defeat your opponent, the higher your score will be. In each round, you need to score three points to win. During the game, you will only have a maximum of five attempts to block misses. If you have not reached the last level and have finished the specified turn, you will play again from the first round.There's no need for too many complicated strategies; all you can do is focus on adapting in time to unpredictable balls. The pace of the round will gradually increase with each level, increasing the challenge.How to PlayTo serve, move the mouse to direct the racket to the ball position and press the key.
