Geometry Dash Silent Circles is a super hard level that is not officially rated. It's a collaboration between many creators and is considered an extremely challenging and almost impossible level. It's likely a modified version of Cyrillic and Sailent's Silent Circles. The level used to be ranked among the top 1-5 hardest levels before it was made slightly easier. Its difficulty is now based on the top 40 levels listed on the Geometry Dash Demonlist.
