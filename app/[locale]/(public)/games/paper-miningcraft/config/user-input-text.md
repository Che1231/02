About: Paper Miningcraft
Paper Minecraft is an online 2D version of the popular sandbox game Minecraft made by Mojang Studios. Select a game mode, skin, and explore a blocky pixelated world just like in Minecraft.

Minecraft is a 3D game launched in 2009 by Mojang Studios. The game has rapidly gained popularity since its release and become one of the most popular video games of all time. If you're a big fan of Minecraft, you will surely love this game!

Your mission may vary depending on your game mode. In the Survival mode, you need to gather resources, make tools, build structures, and try to stay alive in the wild.
Or you can try the Peaceful mode and enjoy building stuff.

The Creative mode lets players use their imagination to create a new world and show off their creativity.

Before starting a game, you can also choose different World types from Default to Flat, and different character skins.

There's no need to worry about losing all of the stuffs you are building after closing the browser. You can save the game progress by pressing the O (letter) on your keyboard.

With Paper Minecraft, you can enjoy the legendary Minecraft game right in your browser online without having to install different files. No download or installation is required! So it's a perfect choice for those who feel the 3D game is too big for a game session.

Step into the world of Paper Minecraft and build whatever you want, fight against spawns, or challenge your creativity. Have fun!

Game FAQ

Who made Paper Minecraft?

Paper Minecraft was created by Griffpatch, a Youtuber and Scratch developer. This game was made using Scratch.

Is Paper Minecraft similar to the original Minecraft?

Minecraft is a legendary open-world video game created by Mojang Studios. Paper Minecraft is a 2D version of the original game. It has the same game mechanism, tools, materials, and features as the original one.

Instructions
How to play Paper Minecraft

WASD or arrow keys to move, jump, or swim
Left-click to dig or place blocks
E to open inventory or chest
F to eat the held item
Space bar to drop or split an item from a stack
O to save progress
P to pause and Space to resume
