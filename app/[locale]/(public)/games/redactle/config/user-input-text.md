Redactle will put you in the role of a newspaper editor to find the topic of a hidden article which is a random Wikipedia article that has words hidden. Because there aren't many more cues provided, such as articles, punctuation, prepositions, and a few minor conjunctions, this exercise is challenging. Additionally, you are required to research the topic or field that the article focuses on while trying to win your game. This necessitates making numerous efforts to identify the article's subject and inferring associated keywords.Guide to PlayWhen a word is successfully predicted, it will appear on the right side of the screen and simultaneously fill in all the spaces where it was originally used in the text. In other words, the more terms you correctly guess, the more you will comprehend the article's content. When there are enough words found, you can infer what the article's topic is. When you find the right topic, the game ends.Similar Word-Guessing GamesSedecordleWord HurdleOutspell
