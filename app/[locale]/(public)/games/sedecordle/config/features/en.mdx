  <div className="w-full md:w-1/2">
    <div className="grid grid-cols-1 gap-2 md:gap-4 mt-0">
      <div className="bg-gray-800 p-2 md:p-4 rounded-lg">
        <h3 className="text-primary font-bold mb-2">Gameplay Breakdown</h3>
        <div className="text-primary-foreground">
The rules are deceptively simple. You are given sixteen grids, each representing a secret word.  You type your 5-letter guess. You use the grid hints (green for correct letter and position, yellow for correct letter, wrong position) to crack them. The pressure is palpable from beginning to end. Your limited guesses are a constant companion, like the ticking clock in a heist film. This is how **Sedecordle** tests you.
</div>
      </div>

      <div className="bg-gray-800 p-2 md:p-4 rounded-lg">
        <h3 className="text-primary font-bold mb-2">Unique Mechanics</h3>
        <div className="text-primary-foreground">
The "Shared Letter Pool" system makes this experience extraordinary. As you are decoding all the sixteen words, the same letter may appear multiple times. The "Strategic Feedback" system lets players strategically interpret the revealed hints. A single incorrect letter reveals potentially correct across all 16 words, increasing the game's difficulty.
</div>
      </div>

      <div className="bg-gray-800 p-2 md:p-4 rounded-lg">
        <h3 className="text-primary font-bold mb-2">Innovational System</h3>
        <div className="text-primary-foreground">
The "Challenge Mode" allows you to challenge Sedecordle to give you the words with the hints.
</div>
