## Core Gameplay, Systems, Mechanics Breakdown for High Scores ##

This is where Stickman Crazy Box shifts from simple fun to strategic domination. Let's dissect the core aspects: *ragdoll manipulation*, *puzzle-solving*, and *level creation* (unique system). Two unique mechanics: *momentum harnessing* and *precise landing*.

**Ragdoll Mastery**: The stickman's floppy nature isn't a bug; it's a FEATURE. Learn to anticipate his movements. Can you anticipate his ragdoll-ness? Can you control the madness? This is the crazy box part of the stickman crazy box. Can you embrace it? Find the sweet spot. Exploit the chaos.

> I remember one level where I thought I was stuck. I kept trying to jump a gap the "normal" way, but kept falling. Then I realized I could use a well-timed flop to fling myself across! Pure stickman magic!

**Strategic Jumping Dance**: The core operation. Jumping is more than just pressing a button. It's about angle, force, and timing. Think of it as a dance, a ballet of broken bones. When you move in sync with the physics of Stickman Crazy Box, you become one with the chaos.

**Building Insanity (Level Editor Strategies)**: Design levels that punish specific skills. Master your levels. What makes one level more difficult than another? Are there different obstacles that you haven't tried before or any techniques to build more complex mazes?

 To dominate the leaderboards, optimize your coin routes.
 Play with the level editor to understand game physics.
 Use the “R” key to reset immediately after failure.
 Stickman Crazy Box is a challenging game. It doesn't have to be impossible, though!
