Can Stadium, Junkyard, or Tropic turn you into an ultimate champion?Numerous Game ModesQuick Match: This is the practice mode, so you can clearly understand the game rules and controls. You can choose the time limit for each turn. During this time, you need to score more goals than your opponent to win.Crazy: Game rules are similar to Quick Match. However, instead of playing alone, you will have an additional teammate as the goalkeeper.Tournament: Participate in the world season and dramatic knockout rounds. Get through the playoff stages and advance to the finals to win the championship trophy now!Challenge: This is a multi-level game mode with certain goals. You need to complete the level requirements to unlock the next challenging level.How to ControlYou can maneuver your character in Football Blitz with the arrow keys and WASD, depending on the game mode you choose.Other Soccer GamesEmbark on a fascinating football atmosphere with Soccer Random, Soccer Legends, and Head Soccer 2024!
