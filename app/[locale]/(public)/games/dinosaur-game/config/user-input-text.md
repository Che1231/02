The Dinosaur Game, also known as the "Chrome Dino" or "T-Rex Runner," is a popular side-scrolling endless runner game that can be played offline on Google Chrome when there's no internet connection. In this game, players control a pixelated T-Rex character who must navigate a desert landscape filled with cacti and flying pterodactyls. The objective is to survive for as long as possible by jumping over obstacles or crouching under them.
