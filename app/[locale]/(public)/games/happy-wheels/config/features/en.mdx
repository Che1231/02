### Features of Happy Wheels: A Deep Dive into the Carnage

Let's delve further into the peculiar joys of **Happy Wheels**:

The game throws you into a world where physics reigns supreme. Vehicles behave in hilariously unpredictable ways. The levels are fiendishly designed.

> “I thought I was doing well,” recalls a veteran player, "right until I hit a landmine and my character's legs went *that* way".

*   **Physics-Based Mayhem:** This is the heart of **Happy Wheels**. Each vehicle has unique physics. This changes how you will play it. Expect the unexpected.

*   **Level Editor:** Unleash your inner sadist! Create elaborate courses. Traps abound. Share your creations with the world.

*   **Character Variety:** Choose from an array of characters. There’s Wheelchair Guy, Irresponsible Dad and more. Each offers a unique gameplay experience.

### High Score Strategies for Happy Wheels: Conquer the Chaos

Want to become a **Happy Wheels** champion? Here are some tips.

*   **Master the Controls:** Practice. Learn to exploit the vehicle's quirks. Precision is key.

*   **Embrace the Physics:** Anticipate how your vehicle will react. Predict collisions.

*   **Be Patient:** Rushing often leads to disaster. Observe the level. Plan your route.

*   **Experiment:** Try different vehicles. Some levels favour certain characters and vehicles. This gives you more options.
