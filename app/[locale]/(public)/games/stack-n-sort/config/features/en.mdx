## Gameplay Deep Dive

Stack N Sort challenges you to manipulate falling colored tiles. Group matching colors together. Trigger powerful chain reactions. The core gameplay revolves around anticipating future tile drops. Maximize your scoring potential in Stack N Sort.

> "At first, I was just mindlessly stacking. Then, I started seeing the patterns. I began to plan moves five or six steps ahead. It's like playing chess, except with vibrant colors and exploding cascades," says frustrated player after losing his 200 match streak.

The **Strategic Swap (an action exchange tiles)** allows controlled tile movement. The **Color Bomb system (a special tile which wipes out matching colors)** adds a tactical layer. Stack N Sort presents a blend of skill.

The key is to build combos. Look for opportunities to create chain reactions. Utilize special tile abilities effectively. Conserve resources for critical moments. High scores hinge upon strategic mastery of Stack N Sort.
