Sausage Flip is an entertaining flipping platformer game. Over 150 levels require the player to launch and flip a sticky sausage with googly eyes against and over obstacles in order to reach a vertical, black-and-white-checkered finish line.How to PlayDraw a line in a direction from the sausage or another similarly sticky, wiggly object to the right side of the screen, using the white area as your guide. Direct the line in the best way for the anthropomorphic meat to travel in order to overcome obstacles. Upgrade the sausage when you can.
