Blob Opera is a great music and skill game online. David Li and Google Arts & Culture collaborated to create this game. The use of a machine learning model trained on the voices of four opera singers to produce a practical and entertaining music game that anybody can enjoy regardless of the player's musical ability, is what makes Blob Opera different from others.The Detailed GameplayBlob Opera challenges you to direct amusing and eye-catching blobs to compose an opera. This game is really easy to play and simple to understand. Just adhere to the simple beginning instructions that the game displays on the screen. Drag the mouse to help the blobs reach specific pitches that you want and play the best notes as they can. Let's get creative and move those blobs to your preferred musical styles. The sound gets more prominent the higher you drag the blobs. Alternatively, you can extend the blobs to make the sound louder and clearer.Related Music-Themed GamesMuse DashFriday Night Funkin
