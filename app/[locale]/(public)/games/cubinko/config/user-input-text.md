HOW TO PLAY


• Rotate and drag the blocks to move them.

• Try to fit them together to destroy blocks with combinations of 3 or more. The more blocks you destroy at once, the more points you will earn!

• In ARCADE mode there is no time limit, but you need to complete the missions to get more moves and keep playing. In TIME ATTACK mode things change, you run out of time but there is no limit to your moves, try to take the time to make the most points!
