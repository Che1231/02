Stabfish.io is an immersive and thrilling free-to-play io game that plunges you into the depths of the ocean as a formidable aquatic creature. Engage in a multiplayer arena teeming with fellow fish armed with lethal tusks, as you hunt down your prey and evade the perilous jabs of rival players. Collect defeated fish as trophies, devour nourishing food to gain boosts, and establish your dominance over the vast sea.
