Crazy Crash Landing is a crash simulator game in 3D, but not with cars like most of them so far, but with an airplane. Fly and crash with speed!

How to play Crazy Crash Landing
Tap and hold the left mouse button to take off and then move it left and right to steer. Use touch controls of mobile users.
Fly with your plane into the field from above, and take off from the ramp. Aim to fly a big distance before crash-landing.
The more furious your crash is, and the more destruction you cause, the more extra coins you earn.
For a long flight and a hard crash you will get the most amount of coins in return.
Use the coins to upgrade the boost, engine, and bonus meters. This will make for a better performance!
Reach the finish line to crash land on it in order to change locations to a new map.
