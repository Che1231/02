The heart of **Klondike Solitaire** lies in its core mechanics. Your objective? To arrange all cards into the foundation piles. Suit by suit. Ace to King.  Controls are usually drag-and-drop. Clicking reveals hidden cards.
          <br/>
          Core Play:
          <ul>
            <li>Stacking: Build tableau columns (the seven columns) in alternating colors (red and black) and descending order.</li>
            <li>Revealing the Deck: Draw cards from the stock pile (the main deck) to find your next moves.</li>
            <li>The Foundation: Move cards from the tableau to the foundation, starting with Aces.</li>
          </ul>
</div>
