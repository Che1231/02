Basket Champs is your chance to shoot hoops and challenge. Master your skills, compete in tournaments, and see how you stack up against other players.Basket Champs will prove that the appeal of basketball has never cooled with heart-stopping matches. Participating in the World Championship Tournament will put your skills to the real test. First of all, choose a national team that you want to represent until the end of the season. Your task in this exciting sports game is to try to score more points than your opponent in five throws. You need to accurately align the direction and force of throwing the ball to score the best shots.To continue advancing to the next round with more formidable opponents, you need to win convincingly. Each turn requires meticulous and careful calculation. The target basket can be stationary or moving. Follow these important factors carefully to have impressive and accurate throws. Starting with the group stage, you need to be in the top 4 teams with the highest scores to move on to the knockout round. Until you conquer the championship of Basket Champs, don't give up!How to PlayYou track the direction of the ball's throw with a dotted line on the screen. Carefully analyze the position of the ball and the nature of the basket (whether it moves or not). Then, you move the mouse to adjust the direction and force of the throw and you click to make your turn. Note that the farther you pull from the ball, the stronger the force will be.
