Word Wipe is a quick and entertaining puzzle game with a straightforward idea: connecting tiles of individual letters into lines to form words. Letters can be used all around and from any direction. The letters vanish from the grid once you put them together to form a word. The location of the letters above is dropped, resulting in a wide variety of special letter combinations!The Logical GameplayLook for words on the board to begin playing Word Wipe. To remove the tiles, choose the word's individual letters. The letters from above take the place of the tiles as they vanish. Similar to the game of Boggle, you can make words by pairing adjacent letters in any orientation. The shortest word is three characters long.Every level you play in Word Wipe has a time limit, so you must act swiftly to complete the task. Your objective is to get rid of a greater number of rows with each new level. Once you've removed the lines, the game will give you a bomb that will demolish a specific section of your choice.Other Word GamesCrossword puzzles are a type of game that requires your divine linguistic abilities.
