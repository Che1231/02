Rainbow Obby: A colorful world awaits! Get ready to enjoy a vibrant obstacle course filled with tricky challenges in an engaging platform game.Rainbow Obby opens up a world that couldn't be more colorful, with stunning parkour gameplay and unique experiences. Players will embark on an adventure with rainbow platforms and heart-stopping twists and turns. You will play the role of Obby, a character passionate about adventure with the most dangerous challenges. You can choose one of four game modes to participate in. Each mode has its own unique game rules.
