## The Thrill of the Run (Gameplay Deep Dive)

Man Runner 2048 combines the reflexes needed for an endless runner with the strategic thinking of a puzzle game. We are talking about core gameplay here. It's a potent combination.

**Core Gameplay loop:**

1.  **Run and Collect:** Guide your runner, collecting numbered clones.
2.  **Strategic Merging:** Merge identical numbered runners to increase their value (e.g., 2+2=4).
3.  **Obstacle Avoidance:** Dodge obstacles that deplete your runner count.

**Operation Demonstration:**

*   Use simple touch controls to steer your runner left or right.
*   Plan your route to maximize runner collection and merging.
*   React quickly to avoid incoming obstacles.

**Strategy Suggestions:**
*   Prioritize merging early on to grow exponentially.
*   Look for opportunities to chain merges for bonus points.
*   Be aware of upcoming obstacles and plan your route accordingly.
