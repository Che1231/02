Phoodle is a chef-themed game, offering unlimited playability. Inspired by Wordle, this puzzle game is designed to delight food enthusiasts. Just like in Wordle, you have six attempts to guess a five-letter word related to food. It can be an ingredient, a cooking method, a renowned chef, or anything culinary.After each guess, you'll receive hints in the form of colored letters. Green signifies a correct letter in the right position, while yellow indicates a correct letter but in the wrong spot. Gray letters signify that they don't appear in the word. Guessing the word correctly rewards you with an interesting food fact or tip. Every day brings a fresh word to challenge your food knowledge!
