Horse Simulator 3D drops you into a massive open world packed with crazy quests. Live the high life as a legendary steed and become the ultimate horse boss!No longer a role of a breeder in the virtual world, Horse Simulator 3D will take you to the experience of becoming a wild horse. Buckle up, horse lover! This simulation game can explore the daily life of this popular mount. Have you ever been curious about the daily life and habits of a horse? It is not only a game but also an educational experience that helps you learn about the topic of animals in a wild virtual environment.
