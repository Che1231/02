Top Speed Racing 3D is a fascinating car racing game where you can just speed around looking for some cool tasks to complete like a professional pilot. Hop on this amazing luxury sports car and speed, drift and jump all over the streets of this free online game on Silvergames.com.

Look for challenges to start earning some money, get speeding tickets which will grant you money instead, perform the largest drifts possible and jump from huge ramps. Do you think you can unlock them all? Try it now and have fun playing Top Speed Racing 3D!
