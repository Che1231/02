Fireboy and Watergirl is a well-known two-player game series. In this game, you control two characters of a team to escape a mysterious forest temple. The two characters in Fireboy and Watergirl have different strengths and limitations, so they must work together to combat the elements. Every new version has entertaining new challenges, creative puzzles, and clever mechanics that send the fire and water combo on an exciting new adventure.How to ControlThe arrow keys are used to move Fireboy, while the A, W, and D keys are used to move Watergirl. Don't let Fireboy go in the water or let Watergirl get burned in the flames since fire and water are elements that don't mix.
