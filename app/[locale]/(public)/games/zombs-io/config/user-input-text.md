Zombs.io combines elements of Minecraft and zombies to create an exciting tower-defense IO game. Construct your base and protect it from relentless waves of zombies. Enhance and fortify your base to withstand the increasingly challenging waves. Test your survival skills and see how many nights you can endure.Join forces with friends to create a formidable base together. However, keep in mind that being in a party attracts more zombies than usual. Exercise caution and strategic planning while partying up with friends.
