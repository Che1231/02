Little Alchemy 2 is an engaging online crafting game. Your objective is to combine various starting elements to discover new items. Begin your journey with the four classical elements: earth, water, air, and fire. With these elements, you'll unlock a vast array of new discoveries. Throughout the game, you'll encounter four types of items:Basic items: These are either given to you at the start or unlocked by meeting specific conditions.Unlockable items: Some items can only be obtained by reaching certain milestones or finding related items.Final items: These items cannot be further crafted and won't appear in your inventory. You can find them in the encyclopedia.Depleted items: Initially regular items, they disappear from your workspace once you've discovered all possible combinations.Use your scientific knowledge and common sense to unravel the correct combinations. Keep in mind that some combinations may be less obvious than others.
