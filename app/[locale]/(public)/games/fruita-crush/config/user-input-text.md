Fruita Crush explodes with juicy fun! Test your matching skills on a vibrant cascade of fruits. Can you conquer this deliciously addictive game?Fruita Crush welcomes you to a journey to explore beautiful lands with creative challenges. You will do puzzle rounds with fresh fruits. The player's task is to achieve the required score with a limited number of turns. You need to create a combo of three or more identical objects placed next to each other. With a combo of four or more fruits, you can create items such as bombs, horizontal or vertical detonators, and the ability to clone entities. When the number of moves runs out and there are still these items on the main game board, they will automatically activate and increase your score. You can only unlock the next level when you complete the mission in the previous round.Fruita Crush takes you on a thrilling journey through stunning landscapes of diverse lands. From lush green hills through scorching deserts to dangerous jungles, each place has its own unique challenges waiting for you. So how to pass every level and become a master in this game? The secret lies in the strategy for calculating your moves. Take advantage of each powerful mega crush to smash multiple targets at once, create amazing combos, and master this fruit world. By using your intelligence and arrangement, you will be able to challenge every difficulty and conquer every level in an epic way.How to playYou use the mouse to exchange the positions of the fruits with each other.
