Tic Tac Toe, also known as Noughts and Crosses, is a classic and popular game enjoyed by people of all ages. It is a simple yet engaging game played on a grid of 3x3 squares. The objective is to create a line of three matching symbols (either X or O) horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.How to PlayTo play Tic Tac Toe, two players take turns placing their chosen symbol (X or O) on an empty square of the grid. The first player usually starts with X, and the second player follows with O.
