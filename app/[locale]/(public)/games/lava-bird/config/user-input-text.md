Lava Bird's got your wings clipped and ready for a fiery flight! Test your reflexes in this scorching side-scroller and become the king of the lava skies!Fly with Lava Bird in the burning lava cave and destroy formidable enemies. You will play as a small bird that can shoot destructive fireballs. Avoid the raised blocks of dirt, rocks and fire to attack your opponents. In particular, this game also has two game modes, so you can expand your exploration. Go solo and fight against AI blue birds, or invite friends to unleash intense hand-to-hand combat.Game RulesThe rules of Lava Bird are extremely simple: overcome obstacles and destroy enemies. You can choose between single-player and two-player modes to participate in the game. In a duel, the player who scores five points first will win in the end.The control operation is also easy, so all players can quickly grasp:Single-player Mode: Use the spacebar or the click.Two-player Mode: Player 1 (the red bird) uses the W key, and Player 2 (the blue bird) uses the up arrow keys.Game HighlightsCombination of flappy gameplay and action.2D graphics with cave images with dangerous rocks and hot lava flows.Two game modes with slightly different but easy-to-understand rules.Control operations with familiar and easy-to-master buttons.Other Games with BirdsGames with birds are always diverse with creative gameplay.
