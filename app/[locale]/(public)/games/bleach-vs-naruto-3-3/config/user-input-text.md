After almost 10 years of development the fighting game Bleach vs Naruto 3.3 is one of the most complete of its kind. With a cast of more than 40 heroes, each with their own specific style and fighting technique, Bleach vs Naruto 3.3 promises you intense and spectacular battles! Have you ever imagined who would be the winner in a clash between Naruto and Ichigo, between Sakura and Rukia or between Aizen and Orochimaru? You may find the answer here by pitting the many iconic characters from both series against each other in ruthless arena battles. Learn how to master each character through the many game modes or challenge your friends by playing both on the same computer. Discover the hidden powers of your favorite heroes, their most powerful transformations and improve your combat technique to best master all the abilities of your characters.
